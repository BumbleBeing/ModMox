# A token is drawn as any card is, with its own token art, and fades where it stood

Tokens came on the card track with Prideful Parent (engine ADR 0044), and a token is something new on the screen. A prototype (`prototypes/tokens/`, deleted once this was built) showed three ways to draw one beside the card that made it: plain, as any card is; with a Token Mark, a silver coin at the end of its name bar; and full art, the art running to the card's edges under a dark name strip, as Magic's token frame does. It also showed two ways for one to leave the battlefield. The user chose plain, and fading where it stood.

**A token's face is a card's face.** Its Battlefield Face and Card Face are drawn from its `CardLook` like any permanent's, with its name ("Cat Token"), its color's frame, its P/T box, its chips, its counters and its damage. The Token Mark was rejected as one more thing on a crowded face, and full art because it drops the colored border, so tokens of two colors would differ only by their art. What says it is a token is the type line, "Token Creature — Cat" (`CardLook.is_token`), and the Inspector: View Original is disabled, since a token has no Original (ADR 0033), and State Information's first entry is "Token: Ceases to exist if it leaves the battlefield."

**A token's art is its own token's, from Scryfall.** `tools/art/fetch_art.gd` fetches it as it fetches card art: for each Token Definition a card creates, it follows the related parts of the creating card's first printing to the token named the same (`TokenArt`), and saves that token's art crop in `content/art/tokens/`, named after the Token Definition's file, with its credits in `content/art/art.json` under `tokens/<file>`. Prideful Parent's Cat is Foundations' own Cat token. The presenter finds the key from the Token Definition the Object View carries. Borrowing the creating card's art was the other choice; it would have drawn the Cat as its parent.

**A token that leaves the battlefield fades where it stood.** It ceases to exist (CR 704.5d), so it has nowhere to fly to: Card Flights makes no route for a zone change with no card (`MmGameObject.NO_CARD`), and Face Motion's ghost fades it in its place, as for any permanent that leaves with nowhere to fly (ADR 0027). The graveyard pile never counts it. Flying it to the graveyard and fading it there was the other choice, which shows a dying token as a card that went somewhere. A new token fades in at its place, as any new permanent does, with nothing added.

Left for when a card needs it: a token with rules text of its own, which its Card Face already prints, and drawing the creating card's link to its tokens.

## Addendum: a flip token's art is cut to its art panel

Scryfall prints Role tokens two to a flip card, such as "Wicked // Cursed", and the art crop of a flip card is not its art: it is the whole upper face, its type line and rules text included. So when the art tool saves a token whose Scryfall layout is `flip`, it cuts the image to the art panel at the left of the upper face, below the type line (`TokenArt.art_of()`, `FLIP_ART_PANEL`, measured from the Wicked Role's as fractions of the crop), and saves that. Any other token's art crop is saved as it comes. The panel is taller than it is wide, so its focus point, set in the Card Gallery, chooses what a landscape frame shows.
