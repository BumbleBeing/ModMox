# A look at the top cards opens the Look window, whose Hand button is baked into its bottom edge

Militia Bugler's "look at the top four cards of your library. You may reveal a creature card with power 2 or less from among them and put it into your hand. Put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order." asks its controller a `ChooseLookedAtCardsDecision` (engine ADR 0084). A throwaway prototype (`prototypes/militia_bugler/`) showed two ways for the Hero to answer it on a mock board: A, a Look window after the Surveil window (game ADR 0069), with a Hand button that takes the selected card at once and Done on the Pass Button; and B, the Library window as a search opens it (game ADR 0058), holding only the top four, where nothing moves until the Pass Button reads "Take Savannah Lions" or "Take nothing". The user chose A, and asked first: "The look window should have the [Hand] button baked-in to the bottom edge of the window, so it appears as vertically thick as the button is tall, and the [Hand] button is always located in the center of the bottom of the window even when it's resized." They signed that off, and asked that the Surveil window take the same structure (game ADR 0069's addendum).

**A look opens the Look window by itself.** It is a Zone Window (`LookWindow`) titled "Look at N", N the cards looked at, placed as the Surveil window is, left of the piles over the Hero's side, four cards wide as the Library window opens. It has no close button. It keeps its place and size, moved or resized by hand or not, until the look is answered, and then it closes. It holds the cards face up in one row, left to right from the top of the library. There is no dragging: the rest go to the bottom in a random order, so their order is nothing to choose.

**A button baked into a window's bottom edge.** `ZoneWindow.bake_button()` puts a strip along the window's bottom, inside its border, as the title bar runs along the top: exactly as tall as the button, in the bar's color, its bottom corners rounded to the window's. The button is anchored to the strip's middle, so it stays at the middle of the bottom however the window is resized. The contents scroll above the strip, and the resize grip stays on top of it. `ZoneWindow.size_around()` gives a window's size around its contents with or without one. The Look window bakes Hand in; the Surveil window now bakes Graveyard in.

**A click selects a card that may be taken; Hand takes it into the hand.** The cards that may be taken glow green, the one selected white, and the rest never glow; a click on a card that may be taken selects it, and a second click lets it go. Hand, a steel-framed button, is grayed out while nothing is selected. Pressed, it flies the selected card out of the window into the Hero's hand with a Card Flight, and the hand shows it once it lands, though the engine has moved nothing yet; the Library pile's count goes down by it. Then no other card may be taken, as Militia Bugler takes up to one. Nothing is answered until Done: the half-made answer is a `LookChoice`, kept on the screen as a `SurveilChoice` is. Undo takes back the card taken; one taken back while it flies comes down at once, and leaves the hand. The Hand Strip keys its cards by Card Identity, so once Done has the engine put the card into the hand, the face already there is the same card and is not redrawn, and the engine's move does not fly it a second time.

**Done gives the cards taken, and the rest fly face down onto the Library pile.** The Pass Button reads "Done", SPACE under it, and can always be pressed: Done at once takes nothing. As it answers, each card left in the window flies from where it is, turning face down, onto the Hero's Library pile. They never leave the library, so nothing the engine does flies them.

**The words are the user's**, signed off in the prototype:
- the window's title is "Look at 4": the number is how many cards are looked at;
- the button reads "Hand", and the Pass Button "Done";
- the Info Panel asks "Militia Bugler: you may put a creature card with power 2 or less into your hand. The rest go to the bottom.", the name of what is looking and what may be taken as a search's prompt says it (`AbilityText.searched_words()`). The Oracle sentence took four lines of the panel, so the user's words are shorter.

**The Villain's look shows only in its result.** A card it takes is revealed, so it shows face up in its hand with the Seen Eye, as a searched card does (game ADR 0058). The cards it puts on the bottom show nothing.

Left out until a card needs them: more than one card taken (the choice and the window allow it, the words do not), looking at more than four cards (the window is four wide and scrolls), any text under the cursor on a card looked at, and the rest put on the bottom in an order the Hero chooses.
