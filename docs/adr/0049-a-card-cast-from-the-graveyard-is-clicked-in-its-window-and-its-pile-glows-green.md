# A card cast from the graveyard is clicked in its window, and its pile glows green

The Hero casts a card with flashback (engine ADR 0048) from their Graveyard window. A prototype showed three ways (kept on the branch `prototype/flashback`): the pile glows and the card is clicked in its window, the castable cards stand out of the pile over the board's edge, and the castable cards are drawn at the end of the hand. The user chose the first, which is how ADR 0032 already has a card in a graveyard chosen as a target, and an Emblem's ability used from its window.

**The pile glows green while a card in it can be cast, and its window waits for a click.** `DecisionClicks` puts a cast of a card in the Hero's graveyard among the clicks of that card, not of the hand, and the Hero's graveyard among its piles, with the kind of Glow a card to play has (`pile_kind_of()`): green, where a pile holding a target is orange-red. Passing is always an answer as well, so the window never opens by itself. In the window the castable card glows green and one click casts it, "Cast Daydream with flashback"; the window then closes, as one opened to answer a target does (A450), since the cast goes on on the board. The top card of the library the Hero may look at is cast the same way, from the Library window (ADR 0055), and the Info Panel shows a window card's click text while the cursor is on it.

**Creatures to tap for a cost are picked on the board, one click at a time.** A `ChoosePermanentsDecision` is answered with a `CardPicks`, as cards to discard are: each candidate glows green, a click picks it or lets it go, and undo takes back a click. The click that makes the last pick answers at once, so Group Project is cast as its third creature is clicked: unlike cards to discard, nothing is left to look over, and a confirm would be a click for nothing (the user's call, after trying it). A picked creature glows white and is drawn turned, as it will be once tapped: its face is drawn from a copy of its look that is tapped, so the face, its place in the row and its Glow turn together, through the same layout and Face Motion as a tapped one. The Info Panel says "Tap three untapped creatures you control for Group Project (2 of 3)" and offers Cancel, since the pick is a step of the cast.

**A spell cast with flashback says so on the stack.** Its entry reads "Daydream (flashback)", and its full text ends "Cast with flashback: exiled as it leaves the stack." Both players see it. Flashback has a Keyword Chip and a Reminder like any keyword.

Paying life needs nothing new: the life total changes as it does for any loss of life.
