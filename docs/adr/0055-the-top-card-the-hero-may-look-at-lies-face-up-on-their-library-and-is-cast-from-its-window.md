# The top card the Hero may look at lies face up on their library, and is cast from its window

Assemble the Players lets its controller look at the top card of their library any time, and cast a creature spell with power 2 or less from there once each turn (engine ADR 0054). A prototype showed how the Hero sees and uses it, and three ways to show that its cast is Spent for the turn (kept on the branch `prototype/assemble-the-players`): A, a gray Dither Shaded plate reading "Used this turn" across the foot of the art; B, a State Information entry only; C, the whole Battlefield Face grayed and darkened. Graying the ability's line in the Card Text with "(used)", as an Emblem's Spent ability is, was shown too. The user chose C with the State Information entry, and that entry says only what was done, not that it can be done again next turn.

**The top card lies face up on the Hero's Library pile, to the Hero alone.** While the Hero's Player View shows their top card (`looks_at_library_top`), the pile draws that card's face a little down and to the left of the face-down pile, so two backs peek out above and to the right of it, with a small eye (`SeenEye`) on its lower left: only the Hero sees it. It can be pinned like any card the Hero may see (`inspectable_at()`). The Villain's library is drawn face down whoever controls an Assemble, since the Hero's view never holds its top card. While a spell is being cast from the top, the engine hides the new top card, so the pile is face down until the cast is done.

**Casting from the top works as casting with flashback does (ADR 0049).** `DecisionClicks` puts a cast of the Hero's top card among that card's clicks, "Cast Savannah Lions from the top of your library", and the Hero's library among its piles, with a green Glow. The Glow goes round the face and the backs peeking out. Passing is always an answer as well, so the window never opens by itself. Opened, the Library window holds only the top card, with the caption "Top card · only you see it" under it and every card still counted in its title (`PileWindow.show_top_card()`). The card glows green while it can be cast, and one click on it casts it; the window then closes, as for flashback, and the pile keeps glowing while the window is open.

**The cursor on a card of a pile window says what a click does.** `PileWindow` tells the screen when the cursor comes onto or leaves a card that answers (`card_hovered`), and the Info Panel shows that card's click text meanwhile, as it does for a stack entry. A card cast with flashback gets the same line. The Tap Preview (ADR 0031) shows the payment of that card's cast as it does for a card in hand (`_hovered_answer()`).

**On the stack the spell reads "Savannah Lions (top of library)"**, for both players, as one cast with flashback reads "(flashback)". Its full text says nothing more.

**A permanent whose cast from the top is Spent this turn is drawn dimmed.** `CardLook.is_dimmed` is set from the Object View's `permissions_used_this_turn`, and the Battlefield Face lays a gray in the card's own rounded shape over everything on it, which grays and darkens what is under it (`ui/cards/dimmed.gdshader`), so the board round its corners is drawn as it is. Its Card Text is not grayed. State Information has an entry "Cast from the top this turn": "You cast Savannah Lions from the top of your library.", or for the Villain's, "The Villain cast Pearled Unicorn from the top of their library.", the player named through `PlayerNames`. The plate (A) was rejected as one more thing over the art, and State Information alone (B) as nothing on the board saying it.

Left out until a card needs them: a library played with its top card revealed to both players, and playing a land from the top.
