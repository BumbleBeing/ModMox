# Card frame credits

The frame, land frame, crown, power/toughness box, mask and mana symbol images in this folder are CardConjurer's M15 frames (cardconjurer.com), reproduced unchanged. They reproduce the card frames of Magic: The Gathering, which belong to Wizards of the Coast. ModMox is unofficial fan content, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

The game imports the PNG and SVG files as Images and bakes them at half size (1005 × 1407) when drawing a card; the originals here are full size (2010 × 2814).
