These come from the skill's test rounds. They show taste, not templates to copy: a new Potion gets its own idea.

Why it works:

## Turned down

- **A demon's face in the liquid** (horns, fangs, red eyes in a violet brew): "Too on the nose, and doesn't really make sense to have a face on the potion outside of a label or sticker."
- **Fire potions whose flames sat on top of a flat red liquid**, with hard-edged yellow tongues and a muted shade band: "if the flame was shaded better and blended smoothly into the liquid I'd like it better."
- **A jar filled with a puffy cloud bank**, with highlight pixels scattered through it: "It isn't clear what the liquid is supposed to be or represent, and the shading/glint is just lazy and ugly."
- **A discard potion (Mind Hazy) drawn as a violet brew with one wisp or tendril rising from it**, twice: "It isn't clear what a purple tendril has to do with forgetfulness or brain fog", and "it looks like a worm in some grape juice, not a mystical potion that attacks the mind." The idea didn't plainly fit the effect, and the liquid looked like a drink.
- **A copying potion (Twin Fizz) drawn as a blue or teal brew holding two identical bubbles**, twice: "This is just water with two big bubbles in it, not a magical potion that can clone a living creature." What the user had in mind: "a mitotic or yin/yang feel to it with some red/blue coloring", which no rules text would lead to. That is why the skill offers ideas before drawing.
