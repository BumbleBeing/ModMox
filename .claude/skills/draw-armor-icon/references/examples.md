# Armor Icons the user liked, and ones they turned down

These come from the skill's test rounds. They show taste, not templates to copy: a new Armor gets its own idea.

## Mixed: a richer Seer's Cuirass (cuirass)

The same Armor drawn as blued steel with a center ridge, strong highlights, gold trim at the collar, the waist and the hem, and a large eye with a gold iris. The user: "I like the metal shading more in this sprite than the other, but I prefer the other sprite overall as it is more elegant and less aggressive." Give the metal its volume, but let the idea, not trim on every edge, carry the piece.

## Turned down: Stormstride Greaves, both drawings (greaves)

*"Bronze greaves with crackling blue lightning running down the shins."*

- One drew bronze in the rust ramp with a bright yellow sheen and a bold blue bolt with a white edge down each shin. The user: "I don't love these, as I feel like the white and blue on brown clashes in a way that doesn't make much sense."
- The other drew a paler rust bronze, a white-cored bolt down each shin and sparks flying off the outer edges, mirrored. The user: "Still not a huge fan, although I do like the shading quite a bit. The 'sparks' should be more varied in location, like real electricity, and the color of the greaves themselves should be more metallic and less rusty, as electricity hates rust."

## Turned down: a red Bloodbound Cuirass (cuirass)

The same Armor as a blood-red lacquered plate held in blackened iron at the collar and belt, with one ruby blood drop at the heart. The user: "I like the color of the metal, but the design is boring and on-the-nose."

## Turned down: Stormstride Greaves, again (greaves)

Drawn twice more, in polished bronze. One had a different jagged bolt down each shin and three sparks of different shapes, all on the shins and toward the lower right. The user: "The lightning looks funny, like it isn't dispersed randomly enough and was concentrated down to the bottom right for some reason." The other had one bolt per shin, mirrored. The user: "The lightning bolts are far too symmetrical and contained to make sense as electricity."
