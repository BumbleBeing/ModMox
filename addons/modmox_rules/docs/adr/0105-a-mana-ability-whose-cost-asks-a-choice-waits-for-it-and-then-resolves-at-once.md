# A mana ability whose cost asks a choice waits for it, and then resolves at once

Jaspera Sentinel says "{T}, Tap an untapped creature you control: Add one mana of any color." It is the first mana ability whose cost asks something: which creature to tap (CR 601.2h, 605.3a). Until now `ActivatedAbility.pay_costs()` asserted that no mana ability's cost asked a choice, since a mana ability never stopped: it was paid and resolved in one step, at priority or in the middle of a payment.

**The color is chosen with the action, and the creature after it.** The ability is still offered once for each color (ADR 0072). Once one is picked, a `ChoosePermanentsDecision` asks which creature to tap, with `is_for_mana_ability` set and `stack_object_id` naming the ability's source, since nothing is on the stack. Answered, the ability is paid and adds its mana, without the stack, and the player keeps priority or carries on paying. One action for each color and each creature, as ADR 0072 does for colors, was rejected: five rows of the Option Menu for every creature, each reading "Add {G}", with nothing but the Tap Preview to tell them apart.

**`ManaAbilityActivation` is a mana ability being activated.** It holds the source, the ability and the color; `choice()` asks what its cost asks, or null, and `finish()` pays the costs with the answer and resolves the effects. `TurnProcedure` holds one activated at priority while its choice is asked (`_mana_activation`), and `ManaPaymentProgress` one activated to pay, whose new mana then pays what it can of the cost, as any mana ability's does. `CastProcedure` and `ResolutionProcedure` ask the payment's `choice()` before its next step and hand it the answer, so a spell's mana, an activated ability's, a ward cost and "you may pay {G}" as something resolves all pay with it alike. Both are copied with the procedures that hold them. While one waits at priority, the engine counts it as a cast in progress (`TurnProcedure.is_casting()`), so `cancel_cast()` takes it back and the Info Panel's cancel works as for any cost's choice.

**Its own {T} pays once** (CR 107.5). `ActivatedAbility.cost_choice()` and `can_pay_costs()` leave the ability's source out of what it may tap when {T} is part of its cost, so the Sentinel alone offers nothing. A creature with summoning sickness may be tapped for it (CR 302.6), as for any "tap an untapped creature you control".

**Whether a cost can be paid stays exact.** `ManaPayment.can_afford()` gives such a source one choice for each color and each creature it may tap. A permanent tapped for another's cost makes no mana of its own, and one that made mana cannot be tapped for another's cost; while some source's cost taps another, any source may be left unused, since leaving a mana creature untapped for the Sentinel may be what pays. So a Sentinel and a Llanowar Elves pay {G} but never {G}{G}.

**The Agent pays with it last but a sacrifice.** `AiPaymentPlanner` ranks a source whose mana ability taps another creature after a creature that taps for mana alone, and before a Treasure. Its `choose_tapped()` answers the choice: first a creature with no {T} ability of its own, which could make mana or do something else, then the least valuable (`AiBoardEvaluator.permanent_value()`), so one with summoning sickness first, then the oldest. The Agent answers it so without simulating (`Reason.PAYMENT_PLAN`), as it answers each step of a payment. Auto-tap pays the Hero with the same planner, so the presenter answers the choice of an ability Auto-tap activated, and the Tap Preview shows the creature tapped beside the Sentinel; a Hero paying by hand, or activating it at priority, is asked.

The Info Panel asks in the words of a cost's tap, naming the source: "Tap an untapped creature you control for Jaspera Sentinel". `AbilityText` already printed the cost.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): a mana ability with two costs that each ask a choice, or a cost that asks anything but which permanents to tap, such as a discard or a sacrifice; a mana ability with mana in its cost; and an ability that uses the stack with both {T} and "tap an untapped creature you control", whose source CastProcedure would still count among what may be tapped.
