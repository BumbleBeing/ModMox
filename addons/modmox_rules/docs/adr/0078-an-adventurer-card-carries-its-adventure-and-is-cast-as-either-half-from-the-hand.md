# An Adventurer card carries its Adventure, and is cast as either half from the hand

Faerie Guidemother // Gift of the Fae is the first card with two sets of characteristics: a creature ({W}, 1/1, flying) whose Adventure is a sorcery ({1}{W}, "Target creature gets +2/+1 and gains flying until end of turn."). An Adventurer card (CR 715) may be cast from the hand as either. Cast as its Adventure, it is exiled as it resolves, and its owner may later cast it as the creature from exile.

**A Card Definition holds its Adventure as a definition of its own.** `MmCardDefinition.adventure` is the Adventure half: its name, mana cost, card type (instant or sorcery), the subtype Adventure, and its spell ability. `MmCharacteristics.printed()` carries it on as `MmCharacteristics.adventure`, so rules code finds it through the one query (ADR 0009). Everywhere but the stack, the card has only the creature's characteristics (CR 715.4); the Adventure is never in the hand's, the graveyard's or the battlefield's characteristics, so nothing can mistake it for another ability.

**A Modification changes the creature only.** A Run Card's Modifications apply to its base definition, the front half; `adventure` is a sub-resource they never reach, so a Mod that adds {1} or renames the card leaves the Adventure as it was printed. That is the user's call, and it keeps a Mod's text, which speaks of one card, true of what it changes.

**Casting it as its Adventure is a second `CastSpellAction`.** `CastSpellAction.as_adventure` says which half is cast; the hand offers the creature, then the Adventure right after it (ADR 0012), each only when it could be cast from start to finish with that half's characteristics, timing and cost (CR 715.3a). `CastProcedure.cast_spell()` and `mana_to_pay()` take the flag and read the Adventure's characteristics, and the move to the stack marks the spell (`ZoneChangeEvent.cast_as_adventure`, `MmGameObject.is_adventure_spell`). `LayerEngine.outside_the_battlefield()` then gives the spell the Adventure's characteristics alone (CR 715.3b), so its targets, its effects, its mana value and what a counterspell or a Stack Color sees are all the Adventure's.

**An Adventure that resolves is exiled on an adventure.** `ResolutionProcedure` moves it to exile instead of its owner's graveyard, marked `is_on_adventure` (CR 715.3d). One that is countered, or that does not resolve because its targets are gone, goes to the graveyard as any spell does. While the card stays in exile, its owner is offered it as the creature, after the graveyard's flashback casts and before the top of the library, never as the Adventure again; it is cast with its usual timing and mana cost. A card that leaves exile is a new object (ADR 0008), so the permission ends with it.

**Views show both halves.** `MmObjectView.adventure` is the Adventure's printed characteristics, so the GUI can draw the whole card; a spell cast as its Adventure has `is_adventure_spell` and `adventurer`, its card's creature half as printed, which the view reads from the card's definition because the spell's own characteristics are the Adventure's. `is_on_adventure` marks the card in exile. Both players see all of it: the hand's card only by its owner, as ever.

**The AI times each half by what it does.** `AiTiming.characteristics_of_cast()` gives the Adventure's characteristics for a cast as an Adventure, so Gift of the Fae is a combat trick and Faerie Guidemother a creature; the simulation weighs the two casts as any two actions. A card on an adventure is cast from exile as any card is.

**The card tool reads Scryfall's `adventure` layout.** The first face is the card, named and filed for the creature ("Faerie Guidemother", `faerie_guidemother.tres`); the second becomes its `adventure`. The Oracle Data check holds both halves to their Oracle text. Other two-faced layouts are still Set Aside.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): an Adventure that is an instant cast at another time than its creature could be (the timing is the half's own already), copying an Adventure spell, effects that care whether a spell "has an Adventure" or a card is "on an adventure" beyond casting it, and casting an Adventure from anywhere but the hand.
