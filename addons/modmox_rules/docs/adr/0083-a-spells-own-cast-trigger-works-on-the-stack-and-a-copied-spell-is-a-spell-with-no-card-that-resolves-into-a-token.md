# A spell's own cast trigger works on the stack, and a copied spell is a spell with no card that resolves into a token

Sage of the Skies says "When you cast this spell, if you've cast another spell this turn, copy this spell. (The copy becomes a token.)" and "Flying, lifelink". It brings three things: a triggered ability that works while its card is a spell on the stack, an intervening "if" about the other spells cast this turn, and copying a spell.

**"When you cast this spell" is a `SpellCastTrigger` with `IsSourceFilter`, and it works on the stack.** A permanent is never cast, so the trigger can't trigger from the battlefield, and by CR 113.6k it works wherever it can trigger: on the stack, as its own spell becomes cast (CR 601.2i). `MmTrigger.functions_on_the_stack()` says so, true for this trigger alone. `TriggerDetection` adds every spell on the stack to the objects it asks, and asks a spell only about abilities whose trigger functions on the stack, and a permanent or an Emblem only about the others. The ability's source is the spell, so it goes on the stack above it and resolves first, and "this spell" is the spell while it is still there. Writing a new trigger class was rejected: the event, "that player" and "that spell" are a spell-cast trigger's already, and only where it works differs. `AbilityText` says "When you cast this spell", "spell" whatever the card is.

**"If you've cast another spell this turn" is `CastAnotherSpellThisTurnCondition`.** It is an intervening "if" (CR 603.4), checked as the ability triggers and again as it resolves. It counts from `MmPlayer.spells_cast_this_turn`, which holds a spell as soon as it becomes cast, and takes the ability's source out of the count while it is a card its controller cast, still on the stack. Only its controller's spells count. A Value and `ValueAtLeastCondition` were considered, as threshold is; "another" depends on the source, which a Value does not know, and its words would not be Oracle's. `AbilityText` says "if you've cast another spell this turn".

**A copy of a spell is a Game Object with no card, marked `is_copy`.** `CopySpellEffect` ("Copy [selection]", CR 707.10) performs a `SpellCopiedEvent` for each spell its Selection finds still on the stack: an object with `NO_CARD` for its Card Identity, made from the spell's copiable values (`MmGameState.copiable_values_of()`, its definition, engine ADR 0044), owned and controlled by the player who copied it, put on top of the stack. It takes the choices the spell was cast with: its mode, its X and its targets (CR 707.10). It is not cast: there is no `SpellCastEvent`, no spell cast this turn is counted, and no "whenever you cast" trigger sees it, its own included. `is_copy` is copied with the object, is in the fingerprint and in the Object View. Rules code that told a spell from an ability on the stack by `is_card()` asks `is_card_or_copy()` instead: resolving it, countering it, targeting it as a spell, and its own context's source. `is_token` was not reused: a token on the stack ceases to exist (CR 704.5d), and a copy lives there.

**A copy of a permanent spell becomes a token as it resolves; any other copy ceases to exist once it leaves the stack.** It enters the battlefield by the usual `ZoneChangeEvent`, which makes the arriving object a token and no longer a copy (CR 608.3f, 707.10f, 111.13). That token is not "created" for anything that refers to creating a token (CR 608.3f, 111.13), so there is no `TokenCreatedEvent`. It is a new object with summoning sickness, and its "enters" abilities trigger. A copy that goes anywhere else, as a countered copy or an instant's copy that resolves does, arrives still a copy, and a state-based action removes it with a `CopyCeasedEvent` (CR 704.5e, 707.10a), as a token off the battlefield is removed.

**The game shows a copy for what it is.** Its stack line reads "Sage of the Skies (copy)", and its full text adds "· A copy: it was not cast, and becomes a token as it resolves.", or only "· A copy: it was not cast." for a copy of an instant or sorcery, which becomes nothing. The user approved those words. A copy of a card, and the token it becomes, show the card's own art: the Object View's `token_definition` is the copied card's definition, whose Original names the card's file (`EncounterPresenter.made_art_key()`). A Token Definition, which has no Original, keeps its own art.

**The Agent casts another spell first.** One-step scoring stops once a play has resolved, so it scored Sage alone above any cheaper spell and never saw the copy that casting it second would make. When a candidate cast's own cast trigger waits for another spell this turn and none has been cast yet, `AiAgent._plan_follow_ups()` also scores each other candidate followed by it, on the same re-dealt worlds; a pair that beats both its plays alone gives the first play its score. That play is made first, and the waiting spell is weighed again at the next priority, its condition true by then. CHEAP Agents and Flat Monte Carlo are unchanged: a rollout plays the rest of the turn already.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): copying an Adventure spell (engine ADR 0078), "you may choose new targets for the copy" (CR 707.10c), copies of abilities (CR 707.10b), copying a spell for each of something (CR 707.10d), a copy of a permanent spell with "as this enters, choose" answered differently from its original, and a permanent cast this turn with this condition counting itself.
