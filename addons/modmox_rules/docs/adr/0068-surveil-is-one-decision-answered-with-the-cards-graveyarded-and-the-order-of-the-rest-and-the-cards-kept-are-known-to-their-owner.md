# Surveil is one decision, answered with the cards graveyarded and the order of the rest, and the cards kept are known to their owner

Surveil arrived on the card track with Wary Thespian, "When this creature enters or dies, surveil 1.", a card of the Villain deck `zar_ojanen_scion_of_efrava` that the Hero can own through Spoils (game ADR 0039), so either player may surveil. The GUI was prototyped first and signed off by the user (game ADR 0069): a window of its own where the Hero graveyards the cards one at a time and drags the rest into order, then presses Done.

**"Surveil [amount]" is a `SurveilEffect`** (CR 701.25a: look at the top N cards of your library, then put any number of them into your graveyard and the rest on top of your library in any order), with an `amount` Value, as a draw has. It is built for any amount, not only 1: the decision is the same shape for one card and for several, and the window was designed and approved for several. The player who surveils is the effect's controller. "Target player surveils" and "whenever you surveil" (CR 701.25c) are left out until a card needs them.

**"When this creature enters or dies" is two triggered abilities**, one `ZoneChangeTrigger` for each, with the same effect: one trigger cannot be both "to the battlefield from anywhere" and "from the battlefield to a graveyard". `AbilityText` words them as two lines, "When this creature enters, surveil 1." and "When this creature dies, surveil 1.", which mean the same as the Oracle text (ADR 0023); merging them into one line was offered and declined. Nothing in the game tells one ability from two.

**The surveil is one decision, `SurveilDecision`, asked as the effect begins** (`MmEffect.decision_as_it_begins()`, ADR 0057). It names the cards looked at, top first: the top `amount`, or every card when the library holds fewer. With an empty library, or an amount of 0 (CR 701.25b), nothing is asked and nothing happens. The answer is two lists (ADR 0016): the cards put into the graveyard, in the order they go, so the last is the newest there, then the cards kept, top first. Every card looked at is in exactly one of them. The cards graveyarded move with `ZoneChangeEvent`s whose reason is `SURVEIL`, and the cards kept are put on top with a `LibraryTopOrderedEvent`, all in one batch, as CR 701.25a is one action: a trigger on cards put into a graveyard sees them together.

One answer at the end, given with Done, was chosen over a step for each card sent to the graveyard. Each step would have moved a card out of the library, an undo barrier (ADR 0015) needing an exception of its own; it would have split one action into several batches; and the log and the Agent would have had a decision for each card. The screen keeps the half-made answer and takes a card back itself, as it does for an order of triggers.

**The cards kept are Known Cards to their owner** (ADR 0057). `LibraryTopOrderedEvent` puts them on top without moving them out of the library, so they keep their objects (ADR 0008 is about zone changes), and marks each known to its owner until the library is shuffled or the card leaves it. The owner's Player View therefore keeps showing them, the Hero's Library window draws them face up, and a Simulation Copy leaves them in place, so an Agent knows its next draw. The other player learns how many were kept, never which.

**While the surveil is pending, its player sees the cards looked at.** Nothing new is kept on the game state: `MmEngine` reads the pending decision, as it does for a search, and `MmPlayerView.build()` shows those cards of the viewer's own library, the other player seeing none. `randomize_hidden_cards()` leaves them in place for a Simulation Copy made by that player while it is pending. Asking it is an undo barrier: hidden information was revealed. The decision names its cards in `describe()`, so the fingerprint tells two surveils apart.

**The Agent answers it by its lands** (ADR 0020). From the top down, it keeps a land while the lands it controls and holds, and those it keeps above, number fewer than six (`AiAgent.SURVEIL_LANDS`, as many as it keeps when it discards), and a spell it could cast with one land more than that; it graveyards the rest. Lands it keeps go on top while it is short of lands, then spells, cheapest first. A FULL Agent answers the same way: one-step simulation sees neither the library's top nor the graveyard, so it would score every answer alike.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): surveilling another player's library, "whenever you surveil", scry, and a graveyard-minded Agent that keeps a card for the graveyard.
