# An ability that works from the hand says so, and discarding its card is a cost

Cycling arrived on the card track with Lightshield Parry: "Cycling {2} ({2}, Discard this card: Draw a card.)" (CR 702.29a). It is the first ability activated from a hand, and the first cost that moves its own source. Topiary Panther's basic landcycling came next on the same two things.

**`ActivatedAbility.is_activated_from_hand` says where an ability works.** CR 113.6 has an activated ability work on the battlefield, and ADR 0036 added the command zone; a card's abilities work in its owner's hand only when they say so (CR 113.6b). `ActivatedAbility.works_in()` is the one check: an ability with the field set works only while its card is in its owner's hand, and every other one anywhere but the hand. So a creature with cycling on the battlefield cannot cycle, and a Llanowar Elves in hand adds no mana. The field is saved with the ability like the Activation Restrictions are (ADR 0036), so a Modification could change it. Deriving it from the discard cost was the alternative; it was turned down because "works from the hand" is the rule and the cost only follows from it.

**`ManaPayment.sorted_sources_of()` lists the hand last.** It is still the one list of everything a player may activate from: permanents in ID order, then Emblems and Potions (ADR 0036), then the cards in their hand in ID order. `TurnProcedure._may_activate()` and `ManaPayment.pays_while_paying()` both ask `works_in()`. The hand comes last so that every action offered before cycling existed keeps its index, and a Decision Log replays as it did (ADR 0010, 0012).

**`CyclingAbility` is a keyword authored with its mana cost alone**, as `EquipAbility` is (ADR 0041): it is made with `DiscardThisCardCost`, "Draw a card" (`DrawCardsEffect` with `YouSelection`) and `is_activated_from_hand`. It has no Activation Restriction, so its owner cycles whenever they have priority, whatever the card's type.

**`DiscardThisCardCost` is "Discard this card"**, an `MmCost` beside `PayLifeCost` (ADR 0048): payable while the source is in the payer's hand, paid by a `ZoneChangeEvent` to its owner's graveyard with the reason DISCARD. It asks no choice, so it is paid once the mana is (CR 601.2h): a cancel or a rewind during payment leaves the card in hand. The ability is already on the stack by then, carrying its source's name and a copy of the ability (CR 602.2a), so it resolves without its source, as a used-up Potion's does, and an ability countered by Stifle has still cost the card.

**The Agent cycles with mana left over** (ADR 0020). An ability used from the hand that only draws, or only searches for cards into its owner's hand as typecycling does, gets the Timing Tag `SPARE_MANA`: it is considered in the other player's end step, when untapped mana would go unused, and in response to the other player's spell or ability. Considered whenever, the Board Evaluator's worth of a card in hand (A179) made the Agent spend its own main phase's mana to trade a cheap card for an average one. It may still cycle away a cheap trick at the end of the other player's turn; that is accepted until it is seen to cost games. Jodah's Codex later widened the tag to any activated ability that only draws or searches into the hand and is not restricted to sorcery timing, on the battlefield or in the command zone too (ADR 0050). An ability that exerts its creature (CR 701.43), and that the tag would otherwise leave considered whenever, gets it too: exerted in the other player's end step, the creature could block first, and it skips the same untap step as when exerted in its controller's main phase.

**Typecycling is `CyclingAbility.search`**, for Topiary Panther's "Basic landcycling {1}{G}". Typecycling is cycling that searches instead of drawing (CR 702.29e): "[Cost], Discard this card: Search your library for a [type] card, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle." So it is not a class of its own: `CyclingAbility` has one Filter field, `search`, null for plain cycling. Set, the ability is made with `SearchLibraryEffect` (ADR 0057) for one card matching it, revealed, into the hand, in place of the draw; the cost, `is_activated_from_hand` and the list order are unchanged, and so is the GUI's way in (game ADR 0022). A Godot Resource runs `_init()` before its saved properties are set, so setting `search` remakes the effect the ability made for itself, and leaves alone effects set any other way, such as loaded from a file after it. `AbilityText` derives the keyword from the Filter, the noun without "card" with "cycling" attached: "Basic landcycling {1}{G}", "Plainscycling {2}". Like cycling, it has no Keyword Chip, Keyword Icon or Reminder. A subclass for landcycling was the alternative; it was turned down because the rules make every typecycling the same ability with another quality to search for.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): "whenever you cycle" triggers (CR 702.29c), other abilities that work from the hand (channel, forecast) and other zones (a graveyard's), and discarding other cards as a cost.
