# An ability that works from the graveyard says so, and the graveyard is listed last

Sanitarium Skeleton brought the first activated ability that works in a graveyard: "{2}{B}: Return this card from your graveyard to your hand." An ability whose cost or effect moves its own card out of a zone works only in that zone (CR 113.6k). ADR 0059 made the hand the first place other than the battlefield and the command zone where an ability works, and left the graveyard for later.

**`ActivatedAbility.is_activated_from_graveyard` says where it works, as `is_activated_from_hand` does for the hand.** `ActivatedAbility.works_in()` is still the one check: an ability with the field set works only while its card is in its owner's graveyard; one with `is_activated_from_hand` only in its owner's hand; and every other one anywhere but those two, so a creature's ability does nothing once the creature has died. The two fields are never both set. The field is saved with the ability, so a Modification could change it. Deriving it from the effect, a `ReturnToHandEffect` of `SourceSelection`, was turned down for the reason ADR 0059 turned it down for the hand: "works from the graveyard" is the rule, and the effect only follows from it. A zone field in place of the two booleans was turned down too, since `is_activated_from_hand` is saved in Content and its default may not change (conventions, "`@export` defaults are part of the save format").

**`ManaPayment.sorted_sources_of()` lists the graveyard after the hand.** It is still the one list of everything a player may activate from: permanents, then Emblems and Potions, then the cards in their hand, then the cards in their own graveyard, each in ID order. Only a card's owner may activate its abilities there, since a player's graveyard holds only cards they own (CR 404.1) and the list is of the player's own. The graveyard comes last so that every action offered before keeps its index, and a Decision Log replays as it did (ADR 0010, 0012).

**It has no timing restriction**, as cycling has none: its owner activates it whenever they have priority, in either player's turn. It uses the stack (CR 602.2a), and its source is found again as it resolves: once the card has left the graveyard it is a new object (ADR 0008), so a second activation still waiting returns nothing (`SourceSelection` selects nothing).

`AbilityText` prints it as Oracle does: an ability that works from the graveyard calls its source "this card", and a return of its own card says "from your graveyard", in the card's text, a row of the Option Menu and the stack alike: "{2}{B}: Return this card from your graveyard to your hand." It is no keyword, so it has no Keyword Chip, Reminder or Keyword Icon.

**On the Encounter Screen it is clicked in the Graveyard window**, as a card with flashback is (game ADR 0049): the Hero's graveyard pile glows purple, the color of an ability to activate, and a click on the card there activates it. The window stays open after the click, since the card is still in the graveyard and still offers its ability.

**The Agent returns its card with mana to spare.** An ability that works from the graveyard and only returns its own card to its owner's hand gets the Timing Tag `SPARE_MANA`, as cycling does (ADR 0059): it is considered in the other player's end step, when untapped mana would go unused, and in response to the other player's spell or ability, not in its owner's main phase, where the mana may yet cast something.

Cult Conscript's "Activate only if a non-Skeleton creature died under your control this turn" came next (ADR 0095). Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): abilities that work from exile or the library; costs that exile the card from the graveyard (unearth, scavenge, embalm); and triggered abilities that work from the graveyard.

## Addendum: a triggered ability that works from the graveyard

Phoenix Chick says "Whenever you attack with three or more creatures, you may pay {R}{R}. If you do, return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped and attacking with a +1/+1 counter on it." It is the first triggered ability that works in a graveyard.

**`TriggeredAbility.is_triggered_from_graveyard` says where it works**, as `is_activated_from_graveyard` does for an activated ability, and for the same reasons: it is the rule, and it is saved with the ability. Such an ability works only while its card is in a graveyard, and never on the battlefield, so Phoenix Chick attacking with two others triggers nothing. Every other triggered ability still works only from the battlefield, the command zone or, for "When you cast this spell", the stack.

**`TriggerDetection.observers()` adds the cards in each player's graveyard that have such an ability**, after the battlefield and the command zone, in ID order as before. A card in a graveyard watches only with the abilities that work there. Only cards that have one are listed, so a graveyard full of other cards costs a batch nothing more than asking their characteristics once.

**"Whenever you attack with three or more creatures" is `AttackersDeclaredTrigger.at_least`**: it triggers once as attackers are declared, when at least that many of the declared attackers match its Filter. **"Tapped and attacking" is `ReturnToBattlefieldEffect.attacking`**: the card enters attacking, never declared as an attacker (CR 508.4), as a token "that's attacking" does (ADR 0044), and only for the attacking player once attackers are declared (CR 506.3b). **"This creature can't block" is `CantBlockAbility`** (CR 509.1a): `CombatProcedure` never offers it as a blocker. It is no keyword.

`AbilityText` calls the source of such an ability "this card", and its return says "from your graveyard", as for an activated one. On the Encounter Screen nothing is new: the trigger goes on the stack from a card in the graveyard, its "you may pay" is asked in the Info Panel (game ADR 0060), and the card enters attacking as a token does. The Agent's yes or no is simulated, its blockers are only those the decision offers, and the Board Evaluator values a creature that can't block a little less.
