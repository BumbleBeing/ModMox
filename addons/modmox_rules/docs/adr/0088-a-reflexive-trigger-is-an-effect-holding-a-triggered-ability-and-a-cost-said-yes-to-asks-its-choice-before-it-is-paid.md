# A reflexive trigger is an effect holding a triggered ability, and a cost said yes to asks its choice before it is paid

Kishla Trawlers brought the first reflexive triggered ability: "When this creature enters, you may exile a creature card from your graveyard. When you do, return target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand." CR 603.12: a resolving spell or ability may create a triggered ability that triggers "when [a player] does" the action it offers. It triggers then, and is put on the stack the next time a player would receive priority, with its targets chosen as any triggered ability's are. So the target is chosen after the creature card is exiled, the opponent may respond to it, and a card returned is one still there.

**"When you do, [ability]" is a `ReflexiveTriggerEffect`, an effect holding a `TriggeredAbility` with no trigger.** As it happens it performs an `AbilityTriggeredEvent` with a `PendingTrigger` for that ability, and from there it is like any other: the trigger procedure orders it with the other pending triggers, asks its mode and targets as it goes on the stack, and removes it when none can be chosen (CR 603.3c-d). A `PendingTrigger` already holds the ability itself and the stack object keeps it, so nothing needs an index into the source's abilities to find it. Its source is the source of the ability it is part of, as it last existed if it has gone (`object_or_last_known()`, CR 608.2h), so a Trawlers bounced in response still returns the spell; its controller is that ability's controller, and its place among the source's abilities that ability's place. Its targets are its own, so `selections()` returns none: the ability holding it targets nothing for them and can't be kept from resolving by them. A triggered ability with no trigger is never among a card's abilities, so trigger detection never sees one. Rejected: a delayed trigger with an event of its own ("whenever you exile a card"), since nothing else waits for it and CR 603.12 ties it to the action of this one ability; and resolving the return as part of the first ability, which is not targeting and lets no one respond.

**"You may exile a creature card from your graveyard" is a `MayPayEffect` whose cost is an `ExileFromGraveyardCost`**, as Guiding Hydra's "you may remove a +1/+1 counter" is a `RemoveCountersCost` (ADR 0076). Its `filter` says which cards of the payer's graveyard may go, authored without "from your graveyard", which the cost says. The question is asked only while such a card is there, so a yes always exiles one, and "When you do" follows a real exile; it needs nothing of its source, so it is paid after the Trawlers has left too. Rejected: a `MayEffect` around an `ExileEffect` of a chosen card, whose yes is a yes even when there is nothing to exile, which would make the reflexive ability trigger for nothing.

**A cost said yes to that asks a choice asks it before it is paid.** `ResolutionProcedure` asks the cost's `choice()` once yes is answered, and pays it with the card chosen; until then the effect has no answer. `ExileFromGraveyardCost.choice()` is a `ChooseObjectDecision` whose new `purpose` is `EXILE_FROM_GRAVEYARD`, among the cards of the payer's graveyard, with no choice index, and the procedure names the ability resolving as its stack object. A new decision kind was not needed: a `ChooseObjectDecision` among cards in a graveyard was already shown and answered for Speak Secrets (ADR 0080). What "If you do" reads (`YouDidCondition`) is set once the card is paid.

The GUI clicks the card in the Graveyard window as it does any `ChooseObjectDecision`. The Info Panel says, in the user's words, "Exile a creature card from your graveyard for Kishla Trawlers", as Fear of Isolation's cost does, and it can't be canceled, since the ability is resolving. The yes or no reads "Kishla Trawlers's ability: exile a creature card from your graveyard?", made from the cost's words as Guiding Hydra's is. On the stack the reflexive ability reads as what it does, "Return target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand." `AbilityText` prints the whole ability as Oracle does, "you may exile a creature card from your graveyard. When you do, return ...", and a reflexive ability alone as "When you do, ...". The Agent says yes as it does to any "you may" that harms nothing of its own, then exiles the creature card with the least mana value, in both modes, since no simulation sees a graveyard; the spell it returns is chosen as any target is.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): a reflexive ability after anything but a "you may" with a cost, "When you don't", exiling more than one card, and a cost said yes to whose choice is not a card of a graveyard.
