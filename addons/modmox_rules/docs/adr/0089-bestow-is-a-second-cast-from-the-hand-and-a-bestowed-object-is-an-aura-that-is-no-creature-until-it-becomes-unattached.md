# Bestow is a second cast from the hand, and a bestowed object is an Aura that is no creature until it becomes unattached

Nimbus Naiad brought bestow: "Bestow {4}{U}", "Flying" and "Enchanted creature gets +2/+2 and has flying." CR 702.103a: "As you cast this spell, you may choose to cast it bestowed. If you do, you pay [cost] rather than its mana cost." Bestowed, it is an Aura spell with enchant creature that is no creature (CR 702.103b). If its target is illegal as it tries to resolve, it ceases to be bestowed and resolves as an enchantment creature spell (CR 702.103e). Once it becomes unattached it ceases to be bestowed, an enchantment creature again, still on the battlefield (CR 702.103f).

**"Bestow [cost]" is a `BestowAbility`, and casting bestowed is a second cast of the card from the hand, as an Adventure is (ADR 0078).** `CastSpellAction.is_bestowed` marks it, offered after the plain cast whenever `_may_cast()` says the card may be cast bestowed: from the hand only, with the timing, targets and cost of the Aura spell it would be. Its cost is the bestow cost, an alternative cost (CR 118.9) that `CastProcedure.mana_to_pay()` takes cost reductions off as it does a mana cost (ADR 0050). Its mana value is its mana cost's (CR 202.3). The cast marks the spell on the stack with `ZoneChangeEvent.is_bestowed`, so its object is bestowed (`MmGameObject.is_bestowed`).

**A bestowed object's characteristics are made by `BestowRules.make_bestowed()`, first of all, as its own text makes them**: no creature type and no creature subtypes, an enchantment, the subtype Aura, and the enchant creature its `BestowAbility` holds (CR 702.103b). The layer engine applies it on the stack and on the battlefield, before any continuous effect, as it applies reconfigure (CR 702.151b), so an effect that takes abilities away takes enchant creature too. Being an Aura with enchant creature, the spell targets a creature, enters attached to it and is attached and rechecked by the rules for Auras (ADR 0064) with nothing new. `BestowRules.as_bestowed()` gives the characteristics a card in hand would have cast bestowed, worked out anew, for the check, the cast and the Agent. Rejected: a Modification of the card, as an Adventure's characteristics are not one either, and an Adventure-like second set of characteristics on the Card Definition, which would be authored by hand where CR 702.103b says how they are made.

**The two exceptions are where the rules they change are.** `ResolutionProcedure.begin()` finds a bestowed spell whose every target is illegal: instead of not resolving, it performs a `BestowEndedEvent` and resolves with no targets, so it enters as an enchantment creature (CR 702.103e). The state-based action that puts an Aura attached to nothing into its owner's graveyard (CR 704.5m) unattaches a bestowed one instead, and `UnattachedEvent` ends being bestowed as it unattaches anything, so the Naiad stays the same object, a creature, that came under its controller's control as it resolved (CR 702.103f). A bestowed Aura that resolves enters as an Aura, so "whenever a creature enters" does not see it. An object that changes zones is a new one (ADR 0008), and is bestowed only when the move says so.

"Enchanted creature gets +2/+2 and has flying" is a `ContinuousStaticAbility` of the attached-to Selection, as Equipment's bonus is: while the Naiad is a creature it is attached to nothing and gives nothing. `AbilityText` prints "Bestow {4}{U}", without its reminder text (ADR 0023), and calls what it is attached to "enchanted creature". By the user's choice it has a Reminder in the Inspector, "You may cast this card as an Aura for its bestow cost. It becomes a creature again if it's not attached.", but no Keyword Chip and no Keyword Icon: the first Reminder with no chip, listed after the others with its icon's space left empty. The Option Menu of a card in hand offers "Cast Nimbus Naiad" and then, in the user's words, "Cast Nimbus Naiad with bestow", as "Cast Echo with flashback" reads. The Agent reads a bestowed cast as the Aura it casts (`AiTiming.characteristics_of_cast()`), simulates both casts as any two actions, and aims the bestowed one at its own creature, as it aims every Aura (ADR 0064).

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): bestow from another zone than the hand, a copy of a bestowed spell or a token copy of a bestowed Aura, and an enchant ability that an effect could take from a bestowed spell before it resolves.
