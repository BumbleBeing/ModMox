# Seeing the top card is part of the Player View, and a permission to cast from it is spent once each turn on its permanent

Casting from the library arrived on the card track with Assemble the Players: "You may look at the top card of your library any time. Once each turn, you may cast a creature spell with power 2 or less from the top of your library." The Villain `arahbo_the_first_fang` plays one, and the Hero can own it through Spoils (game ADR 0039), so either player may use it. It is the first card that shows a player part of a library it did not put there, and the first way to cast from the library. A prototype of how the Hero sees and uses it was signed off first (game ADR 0055).

**`LookAtTopOfLibraryAbility` is a static ability with no fields, and seeing the top card is worked out as the view is built.** `MmGameState.sees_library_top(player_id)` is true while that player controls a battlefield permanent with it. `seen_library_ids(owner_id, viewer_id)` gives the cards of a library its viewer sees: their Known Cards, and the top card of their own library while they may look at it. `MmPlayerView.build()` shows exactly those, and `looks_at_library_top` tells the GUI that the viewer's own top card is shown. Looking makes no Known Card: the top card is seen only while the ability lasts, a draw or a shuffle shows the new top card at once, and nothing in the game state records that it was seen. Marking each top card known as it came to the top was rejected: it would need an event for every way a card reaches the top, and a card that left the top would stay known, which the rules do not ask for. The other player's view is unchanged, whoever controls such a permanent, and `library_contents` (ADR 0052) still says nothing of order.

**A Simulation Copy re-deals exactly what the view hides (ADR 0020).** `randomize_hidden_cards()` re-dealt the viewer's whole library, so an Agent that could see its top card would have imagined another one there, and cast a different card on its copies. It now keeps in place every library card the viewer's Player View shows, the seen top card and Known Cards alike, and re-deals the rest. That also mends an older gap: a card the viewer put on the bottom was re-dealt though they knew it. The other player's hand and library are re-dealt as before, their top card included whoever controls an Assemble.

**`CastFromTopOfLibraryAbility` is a permission, a static ability with a `filter`.** It lets its controller cast the top card of their own library when that card matches the Filter (null for any), with its card type's usual timing and for its mana cost less any reduction (ADR 0050). "Once each turn" is part of the class (ADR 0001); a flag can come with a card that lacks it. `CastProcedure.top_of_library_permission()` finds what allows a cast: the permanent or Emblem the player controls, lowest object ID first, then the lowest place among its abilities, whose permission matches and is not Spent this turn. `TurnProcedure._may_cast()` and `CastProcedure.cast_spell()` both ask it, as both ask `mana_to_pay()`. The top card is offered as a `CastSpellAction` after the graveyard's casts and before abilities (ADR 0012); where the card is tells the cast what it is. A land on top is never cast (CR 305.9), and is not played either, since land plays come only from the hand.

**The permission is used as the cast begins (CR 601.3) and never asked again.** `cast_spell()` performs a `CastPermissionUsedEvent` before the card moves, which records on the permission's Game Object the Game Turn it was used in (`permission_used_game_turn`, by ability index) and the name of the spell cast (`permission_used_spell_name`, kept for presentation as an ability's `source_name` is, since the spell is gone once it leaves the stack). A permission is Spent while its Game Turn is the current one, so nothing clears it as a turn begins, and "each turn" includes the other player's. Two permanents with it give two casts, each Spent on its own object. One that leaves and returns is a new object that may be used again (ADR 0008). The event is part of the cast, so canceling the cast or rewinding it (CR 728) leaves the permission unspent. Both maps are copied, in the fingerprint, and replayed with the decisions; the Object View shows `permissions_used_this_turn`, each with the spell's name, to both players.

**The spell records where it came from.** A `ZoneChangeEvent` that casts a card from a library marks the new object `cast_from_library`, copied, in the fingerprint and in the Object View for both players, as `cast_with_flashback` is (ADR 0048). It needs no field of its own on the event, which already knows where the card came from.

**While it is being cast, its owner does not see the new top card, and the undo barrier waits for the cast to finish (ADR 0015).** Any card leaving a library was an undo barrier, so a cast from the top could not have been canceled, and a cast that got stuck would have been rewound past the checkpoint. Instead the spell is also marked `is_being_cast_from_library` until its `SpellCastEvent` (CR 601.2i), and `sees_library_top()` is false for its owner meanwhile. Nothing new has been seen, so the move from the library is no barrier while the cast is paid for: it can be canceled, and a stuck cast rewinds as before. The `SpellCastEvent` of a spell cast from a library is the barrier, since its owner now sees the next card. The other player sees the spell on the stack throughout, but is never asked anything meanwhile.

**"With power 2 or less" is a `PowerFilter`.** It has the shape of `ManaValueFilter`: a `comparison`, or less or or greater, and an `amount`. It reads power from the characteristics in any zone, so a characteristic-defining ability counts (CR 604.3). Anything that is not a creature has no power and never matches, nor does a player. `ToughnessFilter` is its toughness counterpart, "with toughness 4 or greater", added later for Destroy Evil.

**The AI needed one rule.** The Agent reads its top card from its view and its World, so a FULL Agent casts from the top by simulation, and casting from there keeps a card in hand, which the Board Evaluator values. A CHEAP Agent picks the most expensive spell; of two that cost the same it now takes the one on top of its library, for the same reason.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): casting from the top without "once each turn", playing lands from the top (Future Sight), a library played with its top card revealed to everyone, choosing which of two different permissions is used, and casting from other zones (madness, escape).
