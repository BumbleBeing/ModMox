# A cost reduction takes mana off the cost being paid, as the cast begins

Cost reductions arrived with Claws Out, for the Villain Arahbo, the First Fang's deck: "Affinity for Cats (This spell costs {1} less to cast for each Cat you control.)" (CR 702.41a). It is the first time a spell costs anything but what is printed on it or its flashback cost.

**A cost reduction is a `CostReductionAbility`, a static ability of the spell: "This spell costs {1} less to cast for each [amount]".** Its `amount` is a Value, worked out with the caster as "you" and the spell as its source, and `reduction(state, spell, player_id)` gives the mana it takes off right now, as an `MmManaCost`, or null for none: that much generic mana, never less than none (CR 107.1b). A kind that works its mana out another way is a subclass that overrides `reduction()`. `AffinityAbility` is one: its `filter` says what counts, "you control" being part of the keyword, and it takes off one generic mana for each permanent the caster controls that matches, tapped or not. Two instances each take their own amount off (CR 702.41b). Affinity was not written as a keyword the cast knows by name, so the cast does not change for a new kind. When Claws Out was the only card, the base had no fields, since a general class would have been shaped by cards not in the game (ADR 0001). Leyline Binding, for Zar Ojanen, Scion of Efrava's deck, brought the first reduction by a Value: "Domain — This spell costs {1} less to cast for each basic land type among lands you control.", its `amount` the Domain Value (`BasicLandTypeCountValue`). The user chose to give the base class the Value rather than make a subclass for it, since a spell's reduction is what "cost reduction" means by default.

**A reduction is mana, colored or generic, as the user asked, because some reduce colored symbols (Demilich's "This spell costs {U} less to cast for each instant and sorcery spell you've cast this turn").** `MmManaCost.reduced_by()` applies one by CR 118.7. Its generic mana comes off the cost's generic part only (CR 118.7a). Each colored or {C} symbol in it takes off one symbol of the same type. If the cost holds no more of that type, that symbol takes off one generic mana instead (CR 118.7b-d). Nothing goes below zero, and a cost reduced to nothing is {0}. Affinity takes off generic mana only. Demilich's own kind is not built (ADR 0001). A test-only reduction checks the colored path.

**`CastProcedure.mana_to_pay()` works out the mana a cast pays, once, as the cast begins (CR 601.2f).** That mana is the mana cost for a card in hand or on top of the library (ADR 0054), or the flashback cost for a card in a graveyard, an alternative cost reduced the same way. Each cost reduction the card has is then taken off in turn. Both the cast and the check of whether a card may be cast (`TurnProcedure._may_cast()`) ask it, so the engine never offers a spell it would then charge more for. The rules fix the total cost after modes and targets are chosen. Choosing those cannot change anything a reduction counts, so working it out first gives the same cost. The result lives in the procedure's remaining mana, which is already copied, replayed and undone, so nothing new is stored on any object.

**Mana value is unchanged (CR 202.3).** A spell's mana value comes from its mana cost alone. The Board Evaluator, the AI's choice of what to cast first, the deckbuilder's curve and counterspells that compare costs all still read 5 for Claws Out.

**Nothing outside the engine needed changing.** The AI picks from the engine's legal actions and casts in simulation through `CastProcedure`, so it sees the reduced cost. The GUI's glow, Tap Preview and Auto-tap all pay through the engine (game ADR 0031), and the Info Panel shows what is still owed. A card's face shows its printed mana cost, as a Magic card does. Affinity is printed as "Affinity for Cats", without its reminder text (game ADR 0023). Like Flash, it has no Keyword Chip, Reminder or Keyword Icon, by the user's choice: it means nothing once a card is on the battlefield.

**An activated ability's own reduction is a field of the ability, not a Cost Reduction ability** (CR 602.2b). Jodah's Codex brought the first, for Zar Ojanen, Scion of Efrava's deck: "Domain — {5}, {T}: Draw a card. This ability costs {1} less to activate for each basic land type among lands you control." `ActivatedAbility.generic_reduction` is a Value, Domain here (`BasicLandTypeCountValue`), and takes that much generic mana off the ability's mana cost, never colored mana (CR 118.7a). It is worked out with the ability's source and its controller as the context. `ActivatedAbility.mana_to_pay()` gives what is left, and both the check of whether the ability may be activated (`TurnProcedure._may_activate()`) and the activation itself (`CastProcedure.activate_ability()`) ask it, so the engine never offers an activation it would charge more for. The mana is fixed as the activation begins and lives in the procedure's remaining mana, as a spell's does, so lands tapped to pay for Codex still count, and copies, replays and undo need nothing new. A reduced cost of {0} is paid at once. A `CostReductionAbility` held inside the ability was turned down: a Cost Reduction is a static ability of the object, and this reduction is part of the ability's own text. A static ability on the permanent that names the ability by its place was turned down too, since a Modification may change the abilities' order. Leyline Binding's "This spell costs {1} less to cast for each basic land type among lands you control" is a `CostReductionAbility` with the same Value; the two share it, `reduced_by()` and their words in `AbilityText`.

`AbilityText` puts the reduction after what the ability does and before its restrictions: "This ability costs {1} less to activate for each basic land type among lands you control.", and for any other Value "This ability costs {X} less to activate, where X is [the Value]." The "Domain —" ability word is not printed, as no ability word is. The Agent needed nothing to see the reduced cost, since it picks from the legal actions and simulates through `CastProcedure`. An activated ability that only draws or searches into the hand and is not restricted to sorcery timing now gets the Timing Tag `SPARE_MANA` wherever it works, not only from the hand (ADR 0059), so the Agent draws with Codex in the other player's end step rather than with its own main phase's mana. The GUI changed nothing: the glow, Tap Preview and Auto-tap pay through the engine and the Info Panel shows what is still owed, and the card shows the printed {5}. A spell's reduction is a line of its own, where the ability stands: "This spell costs {1} less to cast for each basic land type among lands you control.", and for any other Value "This spell costs {X} less to cast, where X is [the Value]." The Agent's choice of which land to play, when one enters tapped, asks whether an untapped land's mana would let a spell in hand be cast this turn; it now reads the cost a cast would pay, `CastProcedure.mana_to_pay()`, with the reductions as they are before the land is played, where it read the printed cost.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): other kinds of cost reduction, reductions from other objects ("Creature spells you cast cost {1} less"), cost increases and additional costs, reductions of hybrid, Phyrexian or snow symbols (CR 118.7e-g), an activated ability's reduction of colored mana or on a mana ability, the reduced cost of a targeted activated ability in the Agent's ward check, and showing the reduced cost on a card in hand.
