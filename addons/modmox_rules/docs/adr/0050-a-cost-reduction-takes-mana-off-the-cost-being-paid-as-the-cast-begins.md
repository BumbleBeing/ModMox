# A cost reduction takes mana off the cost being paid, as the cast begins

Cost reductions arrived with Claws Out, for the Villain Arahbo, the First Fang's deck: "Affinity for Cats (This spell costs {1} less to cast for each Cat you control.)" (CR 702.41a). It is the first time a spell costs anything but what is printed on it or its flashback cost.

**A cost reduction is a `CostReductionAbility`, a static ability of the spell, and each kind is a subclass.** The base has no fields, only `reduction(state, spell, player_id)`, the mana it takes off right now, as an `MmManaCost`, or null for none. `AffinityAbility` is the one kind built: its `filter` says what counts, "you control" being part of the keyword, and it takes off one generic mana for each permanent the caster controls that matches, tapped or not. Two instances each take their own amount off (CR 702.41b). Affinity was not written as a keyword the cast knows by name, so the next kind ("costs {1} less for each…", "costs {X} less, where X is…") is a new subclass and the cast does not change. It was not written as one general class with a Value either: the only card is Claws Out, and a general class would be shaped by cards that are not in the game (ADR 0001).

**A reduction is mana, colored or generic, as the user asked, because some reduce colored symbols (Demilich's "This spell costs {U} less to cast for each instant and sorcery spell you've cast this turn").** `MmManaCost.reduced_by()` applies one by CR 118.7. Its generic mana comes off the cost's generic part only (CR 118.7a). Each colored or {C} symbol in it takes off one symbol of the same type. If the cost holds no more of that type, that symbol takes off one generic mana instead (CR 118.7b-d). Nothing goes below zero, and a cost reduced to nothing is {0}. Affinity takes off generic mana only. Demilich's own kind is not built (ADR 0001). A test-only reduction checks the colored path.

**`CastProcedure.mana_to_pay()` works out the mana a cast pays, once, as the cast begins (CR 601.2f).** That mana is the mana cost for a card in hand or on top of the library (ADR 0054), or the flashback cost for a card in a graveyard, an alternative cost reduced the same way. Each cost reduction the card has is then taken off in turn. Both the cast and the check of whether a card may be cast (`TurnProcedure._may_cast()`) ask it, so the engine never offers a spell it would then charge more for. The rules fix the total cost after modes and targets are chosen. Choosing those cannot change anything a reduction counts, so working it out first gives the same cost. The result lives in the procedure's remaining mana, which is already copied, replayed and undone, so nothing new is stored on any object.

**Mana value is unchanged (CR 202.3).** A spell's mana value comes from its mana cost alone. The Board Evaluator, the AI's choice of what to cast first, the deckbuilder's curve and counterspells that compare costs all still read 5 for Claws Out.

**Nothing outside the engine needed changing.** The AI picks from the engine's legal actions and casts in simulation through `CastProcedure`, so it sees the reduced cost. The GUI's glow, Tap Preview and Auto-tap all pay through the engine (game ADR 0031), and the Info Panel shows what is still owed. A card's face shows its printed mana cost, as a Magic card does. Affinity is printed as "Affinity for Cats", without its reminder text (game ADR 0023). Like Flash, it has no Keyword Chip, Reminder or Keyword Icon, by the user's choice: it means nothing once a card is on the battlefield.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): other kinds of cost reduction, reductions from other objects ("Creature spells you cast cost {1} less"), cost increases and additional costs, reductions of hybrid, Phyrexian or snow symbols (CR 118.7e-g), a reduction of an activated ability's cost, and showing the reduced cost on a card in hand.
