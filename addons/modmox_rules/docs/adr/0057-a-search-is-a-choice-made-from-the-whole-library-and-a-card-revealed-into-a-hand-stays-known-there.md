# A search is a choice made from the whole library, and a card revealed into a hand stays known there

Searching a library arrived on the card track with four cards from the Villain deck `steppe_lynx`: Bushwhack ("Search your library for a basic land card, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle."), Ominous Parcel, Oreskos Explorer ("search your library for up to X Plains cards") and Topiary Panther's basic landcycling. Each needs one more primitive after this one. The GUI was prototyped first and signed off by the user (game ADR 0058).

**"Search your library for up to [amount] [filter] cards, [reveal them,] put them [into a zone], then shuffle" is a `SearchLibraryEffect`** (CR 701.23), with a `filter`, an `amount` (a Value: "a" is 1, "up to X" is X), `reveal`, a `destination` zone kind and `tapped`. The searcher is the effect's controller and the library is theirs. The destination may be any zone but the library: the hand, the battlefield (under the searcher's control, tapped with `tapped`), a graveyard or exile; the user asked for every zone from the start rather than the hand alone. The library is shuffled once the cards are moved, whether or not anything was found.

**The search is a decision asked as the effect begins, `ChooseCardsInLibraryDecision`.** It lists the matching cards in the searcher's library and the most that may be found; the answer is any number of them from none up to that most, because a search for cards with a stated quality may find nothing even when they are there (CR 701.23b). It is asked even when nothing matches: the searcher still looks through the library, and it is shuffled. An effect may now ask one decision of its own as it begins: `MmEffect.decision_as_it_begins()`, after any choices its Selections need, and `numbers_with_answer()` works its numbers out with the answer. `ResolutionProcedure` asks it, keeps the answer for that effect, and copies it with itself. A search's numbers are the IDs of the cards found. The hook is not the search's alone: a "you may" (`MayEffect`, CR 603.5) asks its `ChooseYesOrNoDecision` the same way, and its first number says whether its effect happens.

**While the search is pending, the searcher sees their whole library.** Nothing new is kept on the game state for it: the pending decision says who is searching, so `MmEngine.view_for()` builds that player's view with every card of their library face up, and a Simulation Copy made while it is pending leaves the searcher's own library where it is rather than re-dealing it, since the decision names those cards. Asking it is an undo barrier, because hidden information is revealed (ADR 0015).

**Found cards move with a `ZoneChangeEvent` whose reason is `SEARCH`, then a `ShuffleEvent` shuffles the library**, which forgets every Known Card in it, as any shuffle does. With `reveal`, the event is marked `revealed`: every player sees the card, so one put into a hand becomes a Known Card there for the other player (`known_to_player_ids`, as in a library). A revealed card put anywhere public needs nothing more.

**A Known Card in a hand stays known until something hides which card it is.** It stops being known when it leaves the hand, since it becomes a new object (ADR 0008). It also stops being known when any card leaves a hand of two or more cards for the library without being revealed, as Brainstorm puts cards back: the other player can no longer tell which card went, so every Known Card left in that hand is forgotten (`ZoneChangeEvent` does it as it applies). A card cast, played or discarded from that hand is seen as it goes, so the rest stay known. The Player View lists each player's Known Cards in the other player's hand (`known_in_hands`), and a Simulation Copy re-deals only the cards of the other player's hand that its viewer does not know.

**"Basic" is a filter, `HasSupertypeFilter`**, matching an object with a supertype (CR 205.4), so "basic land card" is that filter with `HasCardTypeFilter` for land. It is the first supertype a filter reads.

**`AbilityText` words it**: "Search your library for a basic land card, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle.", "up to X Plains cards", and the other destinations ("put it onto the battlefield tapped", "into your graveyard", "into exile").

**The Agent answers the search itself.** A CHEAP Agent finds the cards that best pay for what is in its hand: for lands, the basic land of the color its hand most lacks, counted over the mana costs of its nonland cards against the colors its lands make. A FULL Agent simulates finding each distinct card it could find (one simulation per name, not per copy) and keeps the best, as it does for modes.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): searching another player's library, a destination in the library itself, revealing a card that is not found (a "reveal the top card"), and cards known in a hand for any reason but a search's reveal.
