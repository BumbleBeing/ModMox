# An alternative cost is a static ability of its spell, and a second cast from the hand

Zahid, Djinn of the Lamp brought the first alternative cost printed as a sentence of its own: "You may pay {3}{U} and tap an untapped artifact you control rather than pay this spell's mana cost." (CR 118.9). Flashback (ADR 0048) and bestow (ADR 0089) were alternative costs already, each a keyword that also changes where the card is cast from or what it is cast as. Zahid's changes only what is paid.

**"You may pay [cost] rather than pay this spell's mana cost" is an `AlternativeCostAbility`, a static ability of the spell**, as an additional cost is (ADR 0086). Its `mana_cost` is the mana paid instead, null when there is none, and its `costs` every other part, Zahid's being a `TapCreaturesCost` of one untapped artifact you control. It does nothing on the battlefield.

**Casting for it is a second cast of the card from the hand, as casting bestowed is.** `CastSpellAction.is_for_alternative_cost` marks it, offered after the plain cast whenever `_may_cast()` says the card may be cast that way: from the hand only, with the card's own timing and targets, while each of its `costs` can be paid and its mana can be afforded. `CastProcedure.mana_to_pay()` gives the alternative cost's mana, less every cost reduction, as it does a mana cost (CR 118.7, ADR 0050), and any additional cost is paid with it (CR 118.8). The costs that ask a choice are asked before the mana is paid, as a flashback cost's are (CR 601.2h), so the artifact Zahid taps can't also be tapped for mana, and a summoning-sick artifact creature may be tapped, since this is no {T} cost (CR 302.6). The spell's mana value is still its mana cost's (CR 118.9c, 202.3). Nothing is stored on the spell: once its cost is paid, how it was paid changes nothing, so copies, replays and undo need nothing new.

Rejected: a keyword of its own for Zahid, since the sentence is the general form CR 118.9 describes, and a Modification of the card, as bestow turned down too.

`AbilityText` prints it as Oracle does: "You may pay {3}{U} and tap an untapped artifact you control rather than pay this spell's mana cost." It is no keyword, so it has no Keyword Chip, Reminder or Keyword Icon. The Option Menu of a card in hand offers "Cast Zahid, Djinn of the Lamp" and then, in the user's words, "Cast Zahid, Djinn of the Lamp for its alternative cost". The Agent needed nothing: it picks from the engine's legal actions and simulates each cast through `CastProcedure`, so it casts Zahid for its alternative cost when that is the cast it can pay.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): an alternative cost from another zone than the hand (CR 118.9a allows only one at a time, so none with flashback or bestow), more than one alternative cost on a card, and an alternative cost with a condition ("If you control a Swamp, you may ..."). The check of whether the card may be cast asks each part of the cost alone, so an artifact that both would be tapped and would pay mana makes it offered when it cannot be paid; such a cast rewinds as stuck (ADR 0015).
