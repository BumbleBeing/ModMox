# X in a mana cost is chosen as the spell is cast, and read from the spell

Guiding Hydra brings three things at once: "{X}{W}", "This creature enters with X +1/+1 counters on it." and "At the beginning of combat on your turn, you may remove a +1/+1 counter from this creature. If you do, put a +1/+1 counter on each other creature you control." It is the first {X} in a mana cost (CR 107.3), the first permanent that enters with counters of its own, and the first cost paid on resolution that is not mana. ADR 0061 left the first and last out until a card needed them.

**X is chosen as the spell is cast, after its mode and before its targets** (CR 601.2b-c). `MmManaCost.x_count` is how many {X} it has, so {X}{W} prints as `{X}{W}` and a mana cost with no {X} is saved as before. `CastProcedure` asks a `ChooseXDecision` for an X from 0 to `most`, the largest X the caster could pay now with the rest of the cost (`CastProcedure.most_x()`, through `ManaPayment.can_afford()`, as `MayPayXEffect.most_payable()` finds it). The answer is X itself: there is no declining, since the cast can be canceled instead (CR 728). An `XChosenEvent` records it on the spell as `chosen_x`, as `ModeChosenEvent` records a mode, so a copy of the game, the fingerprint and a replay of the decision log all keep it; then the payment owes X more for each {X} (CR 601.2f, `MmManaCost.with_x()`), paid one `PayManaDecision` at a time as any cast's mana is. A card with {X} can be cast whenever X = 0 is affordable, since `can_afford()` reads only the generic part and the symbols.

**Mana value counts X only on the stack** (CR 202.3e). `MmManaCost.mana_value()` counts X as 0; `MmCharacteristics.chosen_x` is set by `LayerEngine.outside_the_battlefield()` for a spell on the stack, and its `mana_value()` adds X for each {X} there.

**"X" is read from the spell or ability's source.** `ChosenXValue` is the `chosen_x` of the context's source, 0 for anything not cast with an X and with no context at all. A spell's effects have the spell as their source. `EntersWithCountersAbility` (`kind`, `amount`) makes a replacement effect, generated from the entering object's own characteristics as `EntersTappedAbility`'s is (CR 614.12, ADR 0019), that adds its counters to the zone change's `counters_on_arrival`, so they are on the permanent as it arrives and its "enters" abilities see them. Its amount is worked out with the entering object as the source, which is still the spell while the move is being replaced, so "enters with X counters" reads the X the spell was cast with (CR 107.3m); a Hydra put onto the battlefield any other way enters with none, and with toughness 0 it dies. `AbilityText` says "X" with no "where X is", since the mana cost says what X is, as "pay {X}" does (ADR 0061).

**"You may [cost]. If you do, [effect]." is `MayPayEffect`**, beside `MayPayXEffect`. As it begins it asks its controller a `ChooseYesOrNoDecision` whose `resolution_cost` is the cost, but only when the cost could be paid now for the ability's source; otherwise nothing is asked and nothing happens (CR 118.3). `ResolutionProcedure` pays the cost at once on yes, through the cost's own `pay()`, and then the wrapped effect happens; no does nothing. `RemoveCountersCost` (`kind`, `amount`) is "Remove a +1/+1 counter from this creature", payable while the source is on the battlefield under the payer's control with that many; an activated ability can cost it too. The Info Panel asks "Guiding Hydra's ability: remove a +1/+1 counter from this creature?", as any "you may" does (game ADR 0060).

**The Hero chooses X with the stepper of game ADR 0063.** The Info Panel reads "Choose {X} for Guiding Hydra", with the stepper, starting at the most, on the left of its Answer Row and Cancel on the right where No would be; the Pass Button reads "Cast with {2}", the X shown as its pip. As for any question that spends mana, nothing is selected at first (game ADR 0066). The user agreed these words.

**The AI casts with the most X it could pay.** Whether to cast at all is weighed already, at priority, by playing the cast out with CHEAP Agents, which answer alike. It answers "you may remove a counter" as any "you may": a FULL Agent simulates yes and no, so it spreads a counter only when it has other creatures. `AiTiming` reads through a `MayPayEffect` as through a `MayEffect`.

`AbilityText` also words a beginning of combat trigger as Oracle does: "At the beginning of combat on your turn", "on each opponent's turn", or "At the beginning of each combat".

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): {X} in an activated ability's cost, X with a limit or paid with colored mana, a spell copied with its X, "enters with" an amount worded with "where X is", "if you don't", and asking only when the order matters between an entering permanent's own replacement effects, such as "enters tapped" and "enters with counters", which today asks which applies first.

## Addendum: "you may pay {1}"

Myrsmith says "Whenever you cast an artifact spell, you may pay {1}. If you do, create a 1/1 colorless Myr artifact creature token." **`MayPayEffect.mana_cost` is a cost of mana in place of its `cost`.** It asks the `ChooseYesOrNoDecision` ward's cost asks, with the mana as the question's `cost` (ADR 0062), and only if its controller could pay it now (`ManaPayment.can_afford()`); `ResolutionProcedure` already pays a yes to such a cost one `PayManaDecision` at a time and then gives the effect its yes, so nothing there changes. `AbilityText` says "you may pay {1}. If you do, create ...". The Info Panel asks "Pay for Myrsmith's ability:" with {1} after it, the words every cost but ward's already had; only ward's question marks the Stack Window entry of what it would counter, since this one counters nothing. A CHEAP Agent says yes to mana it is asked to pay, as for ward; a FULL one simulates both answers.

Still left out: a cost of mana and something else at once, and "if you don't".
