# An "as this enters" choice is asked before the permanent enters, and carried on its zone change

"Choose a creature type" arrived on the card track with Rally the Ranks: "As this enchantment enters, choose a creature type. Creatures you control of the chosen type get +1/+1." The Villain `arahbo_the_first_fang` plays two, choosing Cat, and the Hero can own it through Spoils (game ADR 0039), so either player may be asked. It is the first "as this enters" ability: a replacement effect that modifies how the permanent enters (CR 614.1c), whose choice is made before it enters, so its static abilities apply from the moment it arrives and its "enters" triggers see the choice (CR 614.12).

**Every creature type is engine data, a constant.** `CreatureTypes.ALL` (`src/cards/creature_types.gd`) lists the 377 creature types of CR 205.3m alphabetically, as Scryfall's catalog has them. The rules define the list, so it is the engine's, not Content's; a constant needs no file read at runtime, cannot be changed, and is shared by every decision and Simulation Copy. It is never written by hand: the card tool's `creature-types` command rewrites the file from the catalog. Subtypes on cards stay free text (`MmCardTypes`); a content test checks that every creature card's and token's subtypes are in the list. A decision's answer is an index into it, so changing the list is an engine version change, after which a saved encounter restarts (ADR 0010).

**The choice is asked by whatever puts the permanent onto the battlefield, before the move.** `AsEntersChooseCreatureTypeAbility` is a static ability with no fields that does nothing on the battlefield. When a resolving permanent spell has it (read through the characteristics query on the stack, standing for those it would have on the battlefield, CR 614.12), `ResolutionProcedure` asks its controller a `ChooseCreatureTypeDecision` once its effects are done and before it leaves the stack. The answer, an index into `creature_types`, is kept by the procedure (copied in `copy_for()`, since a FULL Agent copies the encounter while the choice is pending) and put on the `ZoneChangeEvent` as `chosen_creature_type_on_arrival`, as counters and "tapped" are (ADR 0008). The event records it on the arriving object, so no event of its own is needed (ADR 0007). Building it as a replacement effect in the pipeline (ADR 0019), with a choice in a suspended batch, was rejected for now: nothing else that modifies how a permanent enters needs ordering with it, so there is nothing to order. "This land enters tapped" is built in the pipeline (ADR 0019), and the move it replaces keeps the chosen type, but the two always both apply, so the choice stays here. It moves there when an effect arrives whose order with it matters.

**The chosen type is on the permanent's Game Object.** `MmGameObject.chosen_creature_type` is copied with the object, in the fingerprint, and shown to both players in the Object View, since the choice is public. A permanent that leaves becomes a new object and forgets it (ADR 0008); cast again, it is asked again. Answering moves the spell off the stack, which is an undo barrier (ADR 0015).

**"Of the chosen type" is a Filter.** `IsOfChosenTypeFilter` matches an object whose current subtypes include the chosen type of the ability's source (the context's source object), read with the layers applied so far (ADR 0018), so a creature made a Cat in layer 4 counts. A source with no chosen type, or one that has gone, matches nothing. Rally the Ranks's second ability is then an ordinary `ContinuousStaticAbility` with a `ModifyPowerToughnessContinuousEffect` over "each creature you control of the chosen type".

**The Player View lists what is in its player's own library.** `MmPlayerView.library_contents` is every card of the viewer's library, sorted by name, with no object ID and no Card Identity, so it says what is there and never where. A player knows their own deck list; the Hero's Creature Type Window pins the types of creature cards in it (game ADR 0053).

**The AI Agent chooses among the types of its own creatures.** Only those are worth anything to it: the types of creatures it controls, then of creature cards it owns in its hand, graveyard and library, ranked by how many it controls, then how many it owns elsewhere, then the list's order. A FULL Agent simulates the best `MOST_TYPES_SIMULATED` (3), a CHEAP one takes the best, and with no creature anywhere it answers the first type with nothing simulated.

Left out until a card needs it (ADR 0001): other ways onto the battlefield asking the choice (`ReturnToBattlefieldEffect`, flicker), choosing a color or a card name, "choose a creature type other than Wall", and changelings.

## Addendum: "As this land enters, you may pay 2 life. If you don't, it enters tapped."

Temple Garden, a land of the Villain deck `zar_ojanen_scion_of_efrava` that the Hero can own through Spoils, is the second "as this enters" choice, and it is asked the same way: before the move, by whatever puts the permanent onto the battlefield, with the answer carried on its zone change.

**`AsEntersPayOrEntersTappedAbility` holds the cost**, an `MmCost`: a `PayLifeCost` of 2 for Temple Garden. It does nothing on the battlefield. Read from the land's characteristics in the hand (CR 614.12), it makes `TurnProcedure` ask the player playing it a `ChooseYesOrNoDecision` before the land moves, while it is still in the hand. The decision is the one a "you may" and ward ask (ADR 0062), with `entering_object_id` (the land) and `entry_cost` (the cost) set, and no stack object, effect or mana cost. A new decision class was turned down: this one already has the answers, the AI's simulation of both and the Info Panel's Answer Row. The turn procedure keeps the land's ID while it waits (copied in `copy_for()`), so playing a land now stops for a decision; its move is still performed with `perform()`, since nothing in it needs a replacement choice (ADR 0019).

**Yes pays the cost, then moves the land with `entry_cost_paid` on its `ZoneChangeEvent`.** The life is lost in a batch of its own just before the move. The ability generates an ordinary `EntersTappedReplacementEffect` for its own entering unless the move says the cost was paid, so No, or any move that never asked, enters tapped. Entering tapped by another effect and paying never conflict, so there is still nothing to order and the choice stays out of the pipeline.

**A player who cannot pay is not asked** (CR 119.4: more than 0 life can be paid only from a life total at least that large; 0 always can). The land enters tapped. A player at exactly 2 life is asked, and paying loses the encounter as state-based actions are checked (CR 704.5a). Undo takes back the land play while the question waits, since nothing hidden was shown (ADR 0015).

**The AI pays only when it is worth it.** CHEAP pays when the untapped mana lets a spell in its hand be cast this turn and it keeps at least `AiAgent.MIN_LIFE_AFTER_PAYING` (6) life; FULL simulates both, with that answer the default. Choosing which land to play, a land it would not pay for counts as entering tapped, and among lands that let a spell be cast, one that costs no life comes first.

Left out until a card needs it (ADR 0001): asking it when such a land enters some other way (`SearchLibraryEffect`, `ReturnToBattlefieldEffect`); unasked, it enters tapped. An entry cost other than life.
