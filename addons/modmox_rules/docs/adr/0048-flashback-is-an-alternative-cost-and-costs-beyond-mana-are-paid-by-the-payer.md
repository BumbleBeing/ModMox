# Flashback is an alternative cost, and costs beyond mana are paid by their payer

Flashback arrived on the card track with Daydream, Deep Analysis and Group Project: "Flashback {2}{W}", "Flashback—{1}{U}, Pay 3 life" and "Flashback—Tap three untapped creatures you control" (CR 702.34a). It is the first way to cast a card from anywhere but the hand, the first alternative cost (CR 118.9), and it brought two costs that are not mana.

**`FlashbackAbility` is a keyword with its cost on it: `mana_cost`, null for none, and `costs`, the rest.** A cost of no mana at all, as Group Project's, is null rather than {0}, as an activated ability's is. The other parts are the `MmCost` list an activated ability uses, so a cost kind is written once and serves both. A card in its owner's graveyard with the keyword is offered as a `CastSpellAction` like a card in hand, after the hand's, with its card type's usual timing; the action is the same class, and where the card is tells the cast which cost it pays. Casting it moves it to the stack with `cast_with_flashback` set on the new object, carried by the `ZoneChangeEvent`, copied, in the fingerprint and shown to both players in the Object View.

**A flashed-back spell goes to exile wherever it would leave the stack.** `MmGameState.graveyard_from_stack()` names the zone, exile for such a spell and its owner's graveyard otherwise, and resolution, a spell that does not resolve and `CounterEffect` all ask it. This is a replacement effect in the rules (CR 702.34a), but nothing else can apply to the same event yet, so nothing is proposed or chosen (CR 616.1); it moves to the replacement machinery (ADR 0019) when something could compete with it.

**A cost is paid by a payer, and may ask a choice.** `MmCost.can_pay()` and `pay()` take the paying player, since a spell in a graveyard has no controller (CR 108.4). `choice()` returns a decision, or null for a cost that asks none. `CastProcedure` has a stage for it after targets and before mana: each cost that asks is asked then and paid as soon as it is answered, so creatures tapped for a cost cannot also be tapped for mana; the rest are paid once the mana is (CR 601.2h, which lets costs be paid in any order). An ability's own {T} is one of the rest, so while its mana is paid its source's mana abilities that tap it are not offered, and `ManaPayment.can_afford()` does not count them: one {T} pays once (CR 107.5), and Mind Stone cannot tap for the {1} of its own "{1}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact". A cost that can no longer be paid makes the cast stuck, which rewinds it (ADR 0015). A mana ability's costs still never ask.

**`PayLifeCost` and `TapCreaturesCost`.** Paying life is losing it through a `LifeLostEvent`, payable only while the payer has at least that much life, so paying all of it loses the encounter (CR 119.4). Tapping creatures asks a `ChoosePermanentsDecision`: exactly `count` distinct IDs from the candidates, which match the cost's Filter, answered with a plain list (ADR 0016). It is not any creature's own {T}, so a creature with summoning sickness may be tapped for it (CR 302.6).

`ExertCost`, "Exert this creature", came later, for Pride Sovereign (CR 701.43): paying it performs an `ExertEvent` that marks the source with the payer, and the untap step of that player ends the exertion with an `ExertionEndsEvent` in place of untapping it.

**Returning a card can put counters on it as it enters.** `ReturnToBattlefieldEffect` gained `counters` and `counter_kind`; the `ZoneChangeEvent` carries `counters_on_arrival`, so the card's "enters" abilities see them (CR 122.6). A token that left the battlefield never comes back (CR 111.8).

Casting from the top of the library came with Assemble the Players (ADR 0054), marked `cast_from_library` beside `cast_with_flashback`. Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): casting from other zones (madness, escape), other alternative and additional costs, a mana ability whose cost asks a choice, and picks for costs other than tapping. Cost reductions came with affinity (ADR 0050), and reduce a flashback cost as they do a mana cost.
