# Explore reveals the top card, and its choice is a surveil of that one card

Waterwind Scout brought explore, through the Map token it creates: "When this creature enters, create a Map token", a Map being an artifact with "{1}, {T}, Sacrifice this token: Target creature you control explores. Activate only as a sorcery." CR 701.44a: to explore, a permanent's controller reveals the top card of their library. If it is a land card, they put it into their hand. Otherwise they put a +1/+1 counter on the exploring permanent and may put the revealed card into their graveyard. With no card to reveal, the permanent still gets the counter. A permanent that has left the battlefield still explores, as it last existed, and gets no counter.

**"[selection] explores" is an `ExploreEffect`.** It works out, as it begins, what explores (its Selection's first object, or for "this creature" the source as it last existed, CR 608.2h), that permanent's controller's top card, and whether it is a land. Then it does its steps in order, each a batch of its own: the reveal, the counter and the move. A land is put into the hand revealed (`ZoneChangeEvent.revealed`), so the other player knows it there (ADR 0057). Any other card is revealed where it is by a `CardRevealedEvent`, which makes it a Known Card to every player until it leaves the library or the library is shuffled. Its steps are worked out from the numbers fixed as it began, so a step done never moves the next one's place.

**The choice is an `ExploreDecision`, a `SurveilDecision` of the one card**, as a scry is one (ADR 0087): it is asked as the effect begins, answered with two lists, the card graveyarded or kept, and so it is shown, made an undo barrier and answered as a surveil is. The Agent needed nothing, since its surveil rule keeps a spell it could cast soon and graveyards one out of reach. It is asked before the counter is put, where CR 701.44a puts the counter first: nothing can happen between the two, since no player gets priority and no trigger is put on the stack within a resolution, so the order changes nothing a player can see but the moment the counter appears. Only the explorer's controller decides, and the card is known to them in the decision; the other player learns it as the explore happens. Rejected: a decision kind of its own with a window of its own, when the Surveil window already shows one card to keep or graveyard.

**The Map is a predefined token** (CR 111.10), a Token Definition in `content/tokens/map.tres`, created as "a Map token" by name alone, as Treasure and Clue are (`AbilityText.PREDEFINED_TOKENS`). Its ability is an activated ability with {1}, {T} and sacrificing itself as its costs, restricted to sorcery timing, and an `ExploreEffect` of its target. Its art is the token art Waterwind Scout's first printing names.

On the screen, an explore that reveals a nonland card opens the Surveil window with that card, titled "Explore", its button reading "Graveyard", and the Info Panel says, in the words the user was shown, "Explore: graveyard this card, or keep it on top." Explore is a keyword action, not a keyword, so it has no Keyword Chip, as surveil and scry have none. `AbilityText` says "[selection] explores": "Target creature you control explores."

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): several permanents exploring one after another, "whenever a creature you control explores" (CR 701.44b), and showing the Hero a card the Villain reveals that stays on top of their library.
