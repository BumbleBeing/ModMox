# A spell cast during a resolution is handed to the turn, and the resolution carries on once it is cast

Goblin Dark-Dwellers says "When this creature enters, you may cast target instant or sorcery card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard without paying its mana cost. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead." It is the first card that casts a spell while something else resolves. The spell is cast by the casting steps (CR 601.2a-i), at once, and no player receives priority after it is cast: the resolution carries on, and the new spell waits on the stack above it.

**"Cast [selection] without paying its mana cost" is `CastWithoutPayingEffect`**, and "you may" is the `MayEffect` that wraps it, as any other "you may" is. Its target is a `TargetRequirement` among graveyard cards, so an ability whose card has left the graveyard does not resolve (CR 608.2b). As it happens, the effect names the card to cast (`MmEffect.card_to_cast()`, which a `MayEffect` passes on only after yes), and only one that can be cast now: not a land, with a target for each of its spell's target requirements, and every additional cost payable. A card that cannot be cast is not cast, and saying yes does nothing.

**The resolution hands the cast to the turn.** `ResolutionProcedure` begins a `CastProcedure` for the card and stops, not done, holding it. `TurnProcedure` takes it as its `_cast`, as if the player had cast it at priority, so every choice of the cast (its mode, its targets, its additional costs) is asked and answered as any cast's is, the engine sees a cast in progress, and canceling it or a cast that gets stuck rewinds to before the yes (CR 728, ADR 0015), where the player may say no. Once it is cast, the turn does not give priority (CR 117.3c is for a spell cast at priority) but carries on with the resolution from the next effect. Running a second, nested cast inside the resolution was rejected: the engine, the GUI and the Agent all read "a cast in progress" from the turn.

**Without paying its mana cost is an alternative cost** (CR 118.9). `CastProcedure.cast_spell()` with `without_paying` pays no mana, so an X is 0 (CR 107.3b) and nothing reduces it. A card in a graveyard cast this way is not cast with flashback, so its flashback cost is not paid either (CR 118.9a: only one alternative cost). Its additional costs are still paid. Its type's timing is not checked, since it is cast during a resolution and not at priority.

**"If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead" is a mark on the spell**, `MmGameObject.exiled_instead_of_graveyard`, put on as it moves to the stack, as flashback's is. `MmGameState.graveyard_from_stack()` sends a spell with either mark into exile, whether it resolves or is countered. Unlike flashback, which exiles a spell wherever it would go, this mark changes only the graveyard. Nothing else could apply instead, so no one is asked (CR 616.1).

`AbilityText` says "you may cast target instant or sorcery card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard without paying its mana cost. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead." The Info Panel asks the yes in those words, as it asks any "you may". The Agent weighs yes against no by simulating both, which plays the cast with its own choices, so it needs nothing new.

Left out until a card needs them (ADR 0001): casting a card from any zone but a graveyard this way, or a copy of one; "you may cast" paying the mana cost; choosing among several cards to cast; a permanent card, which would enter as it resolves; and showing on the stack that the spell will be exiled.
