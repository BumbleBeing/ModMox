# Rules Engine Roadmap

The engine is built one milestone at a time. Each milestone is test-first and fully green before the next one begins, and no milestone adds a mechanic before it is needed (ADR 0001). The exact card list and test scenarios for a milestone are agreed at the start of that milestone, not in advance.

1. ✅ **Skeleton**: the addon folder, GUT 9.7.1, a headless test command (`tools/test.sh`), and enforced static typing.
2. ✅ **Core state**: players, zones, Game Objects with zone-change identity (ADR 0008), Encounter Setup, the Game RNG and AI RNG (ADR 0006), opening hands and London mulligans with Free Mulligans.
3. ✅ **Turn structure and priority**: every phase and step, priority passing, an empty stack, and the turn passing. No cards yet.
4. ✅ **Lands and mana**: playing a land, mana abilities, and the mana pool emptying between steps.
5. ✅ **Casting and the stack**: costs, Values, Filters, targets, resolution, and spells fizzling when their targets become illegal. The first real cards are simple burn and draw spells. Mana costs bring color, derived from the cost (CR 202.2). The game will choose each card's frame (color category × creature/noncreature/land) from its characteristics, so the characteristics query must also work on a Run Card outside any encounter, giving deck and reward screens the same answer as the battlefield.
6. ✅ **Creatures and combat**: permanent spells, including flash, power and toughness, summoning sickness, declaring attackers and blockers, combat damage divided freely among blockers, and first strike, double strike and trample. The first creatures are Grizzly Bears, Hill Giant and Elvish Archers.
7. ✅ **State-based actions**: creatures dying from lethal damage or 0 toughness, players losing at 0 life or after drawing from an empty library, the end of an encounter as a win or a draw, and conceding.
8. ✅ **Triggered abilities**: beginning-of-step, zone-change (enters, dies, put into a graveyard) and spell-cast triggers, intervening "if" clauses, batches of simultaneous events with triggers that look back or forward (ADR 0017), last-known information, APNAP stacking with each player ordering their own triggers, Emblems in the command zone, and the cleanup step's extra priority (CR 514.3a). The first artifacts are Ankh of Mishra, Howling Mine and Ivory Tower. Still to come when a card needs them: "you may" triggers and paying costs during resolution, attack, block and damage triggers, delayed, state and reflexive triggers, and triggers from zones other than the battlefield and command zone.
9. **Static abilities and layers.** Last-known information must keep an object's characteristics after layers, which today equal its printed ones. This includes haste, vigilance and deathtouch, which lethal damage must count. It also includes letting a creature block an additional attacker (ADR 0016's blocker pairs already allow it), and a static ability or Modification that stops damage from being removed in the cleanup step. Cleanup already removes damage one event per permanent, so that check has a single place to go.
10. **Replacement and prevention effects.**
11. **First playable Alpha subset** against a simple AI Agent. The AI's lookahead engines should be able to skip undo checkpoints, which copy the state at every undo barrier. By default the AI Agent chooses to play first whenever it gets the play/draw choice, unless a particular Villain is coded otherwise. Still to decide game-side: what a drawn encounter means for the run (a loss, a win or a replay).

The GUI is game-side work that can start after milestone 6 at the earliest.
